ABOUT HABINATIN

HabiNatin is a promotion site that showcases the local weavers and tells their story, while highlighting the skill they have in making bags made from weaving. This project aims to promote the traditional Filipino craftsmanship by showcasing the locally made products made from weaving.

This is targeted towards Filipino locals, tourists and people who are interested in traditional crafts. The intention of this site is to tell people about these products and the people who make them in order to raise awareness and appreciation for Filipino weaving.
