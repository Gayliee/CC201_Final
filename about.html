About HabiNatin

The name HabiNatin comes from the Filipino word habi, which means “to weave.” Put together, it means “let’s weave,” and that is what we invite everyone to do: weave our shared story.

HabiNatin is a promotional website that showcases Filipino handwoven bags, traditional weaving techniques, and the local communities behind them. Each bag tells you where it comes from, what it is made of, and who made it.

Our mission is to promote and preserve the rich cultural heritage of the Philippines through the art of weaving. We believe that every thread tells a story, and every weave is a testament to the skill and creativity of our artisans.
